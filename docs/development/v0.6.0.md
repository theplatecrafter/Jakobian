# Development 0.6.0 — bounded torus contact and material anchors

This slice advances the nonradial backend with contact streaming and local surface coordinates. The five enabled celestial fixtures retain their existing definitions and gameplay. The experimental torus provider remains separate from native radial `BodyQueries` and the standalone body's configuration parser.

Implemented:

- Periodic analytic torus material coordinates, local tangents, oriented normals, normal offsets and explicit ambiguous/folded projection failures.
- Conservative contact footprints for multiple participants and straight swept-ball corridors, with bounded planning and explicit capacity/budget failure.
- Transactional residency, protected old coverage, bounded LRU cache, build retries and owner/revision-scoped publication tickets.
- A main-thread Unity mesh-collider publisher with count quotas and deferred destruction.
- A versioned ConfigNode codec for analytic material position records, with identity/dimension checks. It does not yet serialize KSP vessels or freeze worlds in saves.
- An explicitly launched native probe using an isolated physics scene for streaming, real rigidbody contact and hole transit; it also checks material-record roundtrip and mismatched identity/shape rejection.

See [contact streaming architecture](../architecture/torus-contact-streaming.md) for contracts and failure semantics. Exact tested hashes, logs and scope are recorded in [v0.6.0-validation.json](v0.6.0-validation.json). The baseline contains the validated DLLs and existing five-body pack; the new test launches through `tools/Torus-Probe.ps1`.

## Executed validation

The Release build passed with zero warnings/errors and **165,348 core assertions**. Three final native graphics runs used the same nine deployed files and completed with zero exception tokens through shutdown:

- Torus streaming: 128 changing requests with two participants, 2,079 builds and 1,983 evictions within the 96-resident limit. A 125.58 m corridor crossing the U seam supplied all 33 sampled contacts. Real rigidbodies contacted the outer, inner and top walls; the axial hole transit travelled 500 m with zero contacts. Oversized coverage was rejected while retaining old terrain. Three material records survived disk roundtrip; wrong owner, shape, schema and nonfinite input were rejected. The isolated physics scene was unloaded, and default simulation settings remained unchanged.
- Historical/stock regression: all six retained 0.2.0/0.3.0 landed-save cases preserved pose and reloaded. A stock Kerbin orbit coasted for ten seconds and reloaded. Original and staged inputs retained their hashes. Only this run's seven private output folders were archived under baseline's `.dev/regression-results`.
- Atlas: native mapped terrain, biome/scaled/query agreement, collider meshes, controlled GPU rendering, gravity/navigation/altimeter/resource queries, camera clearance, landing and reload passed. The human save is `JakobianProbe-JakobianAtlas-20261015-141117`. Captures were retained under `artifacts/validation/v0.6.0`; macro albedo is present, while close terrain still lacks fine detail layers.

The five-body configuration, maps and shader assets retain their 0.5.0 bytes; all four inspected template assemblies are unchanged. The baseline is deployed and closed. Work remains on `main`, with no agent-created commits.

## Remaining limits

A playable torus celestial body still requires general-body construction, a scaled representation, KSP physics-frame binding, spherical-consumer adapters and real vessel/rails persistence. The contact probe's prescribed attraction is a test force, not a physical torus field or centrifuge implementation. This does not add arbitrary expression programs, numerical flight, distributed mass, influence domains, atmosphere or water.

The streaming provider has one mesh resolution and no LOD stitching, terrain maps, render materials or biome/science integration. Cooking is synchronous and quota-limited by count; memory/frame-time profiling and failures under extreme travel/scale still need broader work. Native isolated-sphere contact is narrower than spacecraft, wheels, EVA, docking and multiple-vessel acceptance.
